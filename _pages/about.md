I am a computational chemist with extensive expertise in molecular dynamics simulations, statistical mechanics and thermodynamics, and data-driven methods.
I am earning my PhD in Theoretical and Computational Chemistry from UCLA, where I study the solution phase chemistry of hydrated electrons and solvated alkali dimer molecules.
